Oathway – US Citizenship Test

Delete your account

You can delete your Oathway account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Oathway and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. You may be asked to choose your Google account again.

By email

Send an email from the address you sign in with to vinith0140@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Oathway account. We will delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your Oathway sign-in (name, email address and profile picture).
  • Everything saved to your account: name, interview date, ZIP code or state, settings, scores, progress, mistakes, saved questions and checklist.
  • The study data on your phone.

What may be kept

  • Purchase records. These are kept by the app store under its own policies; we may keep a record of what was bought, as needed for billing, refunds and legal reasons.
  • A record of how many mock interviews were used and when, to prevent misuse.
  • Problem reports you sent about questions, to keep the content correct.

Good to know

  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.
  • Deleting your account does not refund purchases. For refunds, contact the app store.
  • To start over without deleting your account, use Profile → Reset all progress.
