Oathway – US Citizenship Test

Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

Oathway is an independent study app that helps you prepare for the U.S. citizenship (naturalization) civics test. It is not affiliated with USCIS or any government agency. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. We keep it to what the app needs.

Information we collect

  • Your account. When you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Your study details. What you enter or create in the app: first name, interview date, ZIP code or state (to show your own officials' names), language and voice settings, test scores, progress, mistakes, saved questions and checklist ticks.
  • Purchases. Whether you bought the unlock or extra mock interviews. Payments are handled by the app store; we never see your card details.
  • Your voice, only in a mock interview. When you start a mock interview, the microphone is used and your speech is sent to an AI speech service so the practice officer can talk with you. It is used only during that interview. We do not keep recordings. We keep a count and the dates of the mock interviews you used.
  • Problem reports. If you report a problem with a question: the question, the reason you chose and any note you write.
  • Technical information. Like any app, basic technical data (such as your IP address and app version) is processed by our hosting providers to deliver the app and its audio. We do not use advertising IDs.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign-in, saving your progress so it follows you to any phone, showing your local officials, and playing explanations in your language.
  • To unlock what you bought.
  • To run mock interviews.
  • To fix questions and answers you report.
  • To keep the service safe and prevent misuse.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't share your information for advertising.

Service providers

We use trusted service providers only to run the app: sign-in and cloud storage, purchase processing, content delivery, and an AI speech service for mock interviews. They process information on our behalf and under their own security and privacy terms. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Security and storage

Information is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and stored with established cloud providers, which may be in the United States or other countries. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is perfectly secure.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and study data while your account exists. When you delete your account, they are deleted. Records of purchases and of mock interviews used may be kept as needed for billing, fraud prevention and legal reasons. Problem reports may be kept to improve the app's content.

Your choices

  • Change your details any time in the app (Profile).
  • Reset your study progress in Profile → Reset all progress.
  • Delete your account and data: see how.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask any privacy question, by email (below).

Children

Oathway is made for adults preparing for citizenship. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
